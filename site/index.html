FAQ

What do you store about me?

Your Minecraft UUID and name, your equipped cosmetics and when you last played, so other Aero players can see your cosmetics. This site shows the totals and the names of players who are online right now. Turn off "Share profile online" in the client to not appear, or delete your entry there.

Is it safe?

The client never sees your password. Your identity is confirmed through Mojang's own session server. The launcher and mod are open source.

Which version does it support?

Minecraft 1.21.11 on Fabric.

How do updates work?

The launcher downloads the newest mod automatically each time you start it.
